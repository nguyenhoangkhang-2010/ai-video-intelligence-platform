# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AI Video Intelligence Platform (ReelSense) is an open project, and we want everyone who takes part — opening an issue, reviewing a pull request, asking a question, or reporting a bug — to have a respectful and harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, especially when you disagree. Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming someone meant something negatively.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time: give enough context in issues and pull requests (what you expected, what happened, how to reproduce).
- Credit other people's work, and be honest about what you have and haven't tested.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, whether in public threads or private messages.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to harass someone.
- Publishing someone else's private information (contact details, addresses, credentials) without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions (trolling, spam, repeated off-topic posts).
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review comments) — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([@nguyenhoangkhang-2010](https://github.com/nguyenhoangkhang-2010)). Please don't report conduct issues in a public issue.

Include what happened, where (links if possible), when, and who was involved. All reports will be handled as confidentially as possible, and the reporter will not face retaliation for a report made in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take any action they consider appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, such as:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining the problem and what is expected instead.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning, with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for repeated or severe violations.

The maintainer may also remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.
